FAQ

Questions?
We've got answers.

From cost and timelines to ownership, existing codebases, technologies, and what happens after launch, here's what teams usually want to know before working with a custom software development partner.

01

What are custom software development services?

Custom software development services create software around the specific needs of your organization and users rather than forcing your workflows into an off-the-shelf tool. SmartLogic builds custom web and mobile applications, supports product design and infrastructure, and helps organizations continue improving their software after launch.

02

How do I choose a custom software development company?

When choosing a custom software development company, look for a partner that takes time to understand the problem before proposing a solution, communicates directly and transparently, has relevant work you can review, and can support the software beyond launch. You should also understand who will actually work on your project, how decisions are made, and who owns the code and infrastructure when the work is done.

03

What's the difference between staff augmentation and full outsourcing?

With staff augmentation, outside software developers join your existing team to add skills or capacity while your organization continues to lead the work. With full software development outsourcing, the development partner takes responsibility for more of the project, which may include discovery, design, engineering, infrastructure, and delivery. The right model depends on the team and expertise you already have.

04

How much does custom software development cost?

There is no single standard price for custom software development. Cost depends on factors such as scope, complexity, integrations, design needs, infrastructure, and the level of ongoing support. SmartLogic starts by understanding what you need to accomplish so the work can be scoped around the problem rather than an arbitrary list of features.

05

What types of organizations does SmartLogic work with?

SmartLogic works with organizations including healthcare and health tech teams, nonprofits, funded startups, and education technology organizations. The work varies by industry, but the approach stays the same: understand the users, constraints, and real-world problem before deciding what to build.
