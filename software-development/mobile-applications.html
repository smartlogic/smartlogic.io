Our projects

Real applications. Real people. Real problems worth solving.

Here are a few examples of mobile applications we've built for our clients.

01 / HEALTHCARE

Simplifying patient-centered surveys, wherever care happens.

The National Committee for Quality Assurance (NCQA) was developing a new approach to helping patients set personal health goals and track their progress.

To support that methodology, practitioners needed a way to work through surveys with patients, collect information, and follow up on outcomes. And they needed to be able to do it even when internet connectivity wasn't available.

SmartLogic partnered with NCQA to build a digital platform that included an iPad application, API, and web-based administration and reporting dashboard.

The iPad application uses adaptive questionnaires to make the survey experience easier to navigate. It also stores information locally when offline and synchronizes that data when connectivity returns.

"The SmartLogic team was flexible enough to pivot with our needs, which was really important to us, because of the nature of our organization." — Erin Giovannetti, Senior Research Scientist at NCQA
02 / CROSS-PLATFORM

Getting a new marketplace from idea to launch without building an internal engineering team.

After roughly 18 months of research and development, Baltimore startup knctrr was ready to bring its gig-economy platform to market. The founders had a clear vision, but they didn't have an internal development team ready to build it. Hiring and onboarding one would have taken time they couldn't afford.

SmartLogic worked with knctrr to take the application from concept to production in under three months. The platform included companion React Native applications for iOS and Android, a responsive web application, and an Elixir/Phoenix back end.

Beyond development, the partnership involved planning decisions around payment processing, messaging, architecture, and scalability.

03 / TICKETING

Making ticketing easier for passengers and more useful for the business.

In 2016, Baltimore Water Taxi was operating with a paper-based ticketing process. Customers couldn't purchase tickets in advance, and the business lacked the reporting tools needed to understand how people were using its services.

SmartLogic worked with Baltimore Water Taxi to build a digital ticketing system that allowed customers to purchase tickets through its website and boat operators to manage ticketing through a mobile application.

The new system made purchasing more convenient and gave the business visibility into usage patterns it hadn't previously had. With that information, Baltimore Water Taxi could identify infrequently used pickup locations and plan more efficient routes for the following season, reducing overhead and helping passengers reach their destinations faster.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

You don't have to have every requirement figured out before starting a conversation. Here are answers to some common questions about building and improving mobile applications.

01Do you develop mobile apps for both iOS and Android?

Yes. SmartLogic develops mobile applications for both iOS and Android, including cross-platform applications built with React Native.

For many projects, a shared codebase can help deliver a consistent experience across both platforms while simplifying ongoing development and maintenance.

The right approach depends on your users, functionality, integrations, and performance requirements. We'll help you evaluate those considerations before deciding how to build your application.

02Which company is best for custom mobile app development?

The best development partner depends on your product and team. Look for a company that asks thoughtful questions about users, has relevant experience, can explain technology tradeoffs, and is clear about collaboration, code ownership, launch, and ongoing support. SmartLogic works directly with teams to understand what they're building and why.

03Can SmartLogic take over and modernize an existing mobile app?

We can discuss an existing application's current condition, codebase, dependencies, user experience, and priorities before recommending a path forward. That might involve focused updates, new integrations, or larger changes depending on what the application needs.

04How long does mobile app development take?

Timelines vary based on the number of features, design needs, integrations, testing, and platform requirements. For context, SmartLogic helped knctrr bring its platform to production in under three months, but that result is specific to that project rather than a standard estimate. We can discuss a realistic plan after learning about your requirements.

05How much does it cost to build a mobile app?

Custom mobile app development costs depend on scope, complexity, integrations, user experience, existing code, and the amount of post-launch support required. Instead of quoting a single price for every app, we start by understanding the work and discussing an approach that fits your priorities and budget.
